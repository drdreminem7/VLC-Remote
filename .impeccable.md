# Design context

## Target audience

A single Mac owner using their phone at home, often in a dim room while media
is playing on a larger screen. They need controls to be legible at a glance and
easy to hit without giving the phone their full attention.

## Primary use cases

- Pair a phone securely with the Mac-hosted remote.
- Confirm that the Mac service and VLC are reachable.
- Read the current title and playback time.
- Operate essential playback controls quickly with one hand.
- Recover confidently when Wi-Fi, the backend, or VLC becomes unavailable.
- Install the remote as a home-screen PWA.

## Brand personality

Quiet, cinematic, and tactile. The interface should feel like a purpose-built
physical remote translated to a phone: low-light, restrained, confident, and
highly legible. It should avoid both neon “gamer” styling and generic SaaS
dashboard patterns.

## Phase 1 visual direction

- Use a dark, warm-neutral palette suited to low-light viewing.
- Reserve a warm amber accent for the primary action and active state.
- Prefer strong typographic hierarchy, large touch targets, and a small number
  of layered surfaces.
- Use asymmetry and spacing to separate status, media, and controls.
- Keep motion subtle and state-driven, respecting reduced-motion preferences.
